# Sources & References

## Primary Literary Sources

### Original Works

- Goethe, J.W. (1782). "Erlkönig." In *Gedichte*. 
- Herder, J.G. (1778-1779). "Erlkönigs Tochter." In *Stimmen der Völker in Liedern*. [Translation of Danish ballad that created the German "Erlkönig" name]

### Danish Source Material

- Danish folk ballad "Elveskud" or "Hr. Oluf han rider" — Original Scandinavian source that Herder translated

## Musical Settings

### Primary Setting

- Schubert, F. (1815). "Erlkönig," D. 328, Op. 1. Art song (lied) for voice and piano.

### Other Notable Settings

- Loewe, C. (1818). "Erlkönig," Op. 1, No. 3. Art song setting.
- Reichardt, J.F. (1794). Early setting of Goethe's text.
- Liszt, F. Piano transcription of Schubert's "Erlkönig."
- Over 100 other composers have set Goethe's text to music.
